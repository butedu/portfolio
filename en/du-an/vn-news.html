VN NEWS

Overview.

Readers can find, read and save articles. Editors manage content in an admin area with role-based access.

Features.

  1. Editorial workflow

    Manage articles, categories, tags and accounts. Articles go through approval before publication.

  2. Read and save articles

    Browse by category or tag, read on mobile and save articles for later.

  3. Newsletter subscriptions

    Readers follow selected topics and receive email updates when relevant articles are approved.

Technologies.

  • Laravel
  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • Blade
